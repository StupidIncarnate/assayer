## What to implement

Each test asserts the analysis of one file. Before row #3 it saw one `switch` branch per case clause, each with `then` and `else`.
After row #3 it sees one branch per `switch` statement. For each of the three files:

1. Read the specimen and reason out, from the design, what the analysis holds: the one `switch` branch with its `arms`,
   its `armConditions` and `condition`, its `guardPath` (the enclosing `if` arm for `fallthrough-in-if` and `switch-in-if`), the
   guard steps on every exit, and the cases.
2. Run a probe (`packages/core/CLAUDE.md` section 7) to see the real coverage ids and field order. Take the **structure** from
   the design and the **spelling of ids** from the probe. Treat any difference in structure as a defect to report, never as a
   value to copy.
3. Rewrite the expected objects. The arms the specimens use are:

   | Specimen | Arms | Cases |
   |---|---|---|
   | `fallthrough-in-if` | `case:a`, `default` on the `switch`; `then`, `else` on the `if` | the `if` else; the `if` then with `mode` `'a'`; the `if` then with a `mode` other than `'a'` |
   | `if-in-switch` | `case:get`, `default` on the `switch`; `then`, `else` on the `if` inside `case 'get'` | `get` with `size` above 5, `get` with `size` 5 or below, and `post` |
   | `switch-in-if` | `case:get`, `default` on the `switch`; `then`, `else` on the `if` | the `if` else; the `if` then with `get`; the `if` then with `post` |

4. Keep each test's other assertions (parameter types, return types, lines) and update only what the new shape changes.
5. Run formatting immunity on one file of the three: a reformatted copy gives identical coverage ids.
