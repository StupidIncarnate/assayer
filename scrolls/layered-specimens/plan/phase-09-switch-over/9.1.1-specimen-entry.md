## Contract

`specimenEntryTransformer({ plan, files, today }): SpecimenEntry`

- `plan`: the specimen's `PiecePlan`.
- `files`: the specimen's rendered files, `{ relPath, content }[]`.
- `today`: the today ids the specimen matches.

## What to implement

Fill every field of `SpecimenEntry` from the plan. `uses` lists every piece in the plan's trees (entry tree, siblings and helpers), focus piece first, each once, in the order first seen. `files` lists the relPaths, sorted. `today` is sorted and has no duplicates. Parse the result with the contract.

## Tests

- `VALID:` a card plan with no today ids.
- `VALID:` a systematic plan with two today ids, given out of order.
- `VALID:` a plan with a sibling file lists both relPaths.
- `EMPTY:` a plan with a single piece.
