## Contract

`expectedFailuresReportTransformer({ entries }): string`, the text of `EXPECTED-FAILURES.md`. `entries` are `SpecimenEntry` rows (task 1.3.2). The today rows come from `todayStatics` (task 1.1.5).

## What to implement

Write a title, then one sentence saying that every specimen listed runs as `it.failing` until the core phase named closes its gap. Then one section per today id, sorted, each holding the row's `today` sentence, its `closedBy` phase, and the sorted folders that carry the id. A specimen with two ids is listed under both. An empty list writes the title and `None.`. The text ends with one newline.

## Tests

- `VALID:` two ids and one folder under both.
- `VALID:` ids and folders come out sorted.
- `EMPTY:` no entries gives the title and `None.`.
