## What to implement

The checker types every step built on `process.env` as `any`, so `env-steps-type` types an operand from its steps: the raw read is a `string`, `Number(x)` a `number`, a comparison a `boolean`, a `split` an array, and an unset guard the union of `undefined` and what its chain builds. Add: a `template` step makes the type `{ kind: 'string' }`, whatever type precedes it. After a `guard`, an unset variable stays `undefined` and skips the steps after the guard, so the type is the union of `undefined` and `string`, as for any step after a guard.
