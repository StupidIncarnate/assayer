## What to implement

One file, one export (`gtPiece`, `notPiece`, `nullishPiece`). Keep each old file's `code`, generic constraints
and anchors where they still say something true.

| Piece | folder | tag | slots (accepts, position, under) | arms by position | leaf | axes | defaultType |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gt` | `gt` | expression | `value` (expression, value), `limit` (expression, value) | condition: `leaves` | `outcome` | `type`, `level`, `iteratives` | `number` |
| `not` | `not` | expression | `value` (expression, value) | condition: `leaves` | `truthiness` | `type`, `level`, `nullability` | `boolean` |
| `nullish` | `nullish` | expression | `value` (expression, inherit), `fallback` (expression, inherit, under `right`) | condition: `leaves`; exit: `left` (cause `nullish`), `right`; value: `left` (cause `nullish`), `right` | `nullish` | `position`, `type`, `nullability`, `level`, `iteratives` | `string` |

- `gt` keeps the constraint `number | string`: no boolean and no literal union (DESIGN.md "Type variation"). Its
  two holes are the shape `two-args-one-check` (both `value` and `limit` can be parameters), which is why it
  crosses `iteratives` although it has only leaves. Say so in a comment.
- `nullish`'s `value` hole is `T | undefined`. Its `fallback` slot runs only when the left is missing, so it
  declares `under: 'right'`. The `value` slot runs always and declares none. The arm names `left` and `right`
  are the design arms; the report vocabulary maps them to `then` and `else` (task 1.1.4).
- `not` has only leaves, so it declares no `iteratives`.
