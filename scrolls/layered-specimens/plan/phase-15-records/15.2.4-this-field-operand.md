The leaf builder, not `read-condition`, is the seam. `read-condition` reads only the raw comparison facts and has neither

declaration (line 72). The change below is therefore made there, and `read-condition` needs no edit.

## What to implement

1. **A `this.<field>` operand.** When `readout.operandNode` is a `PropertyAccessExpression` whose receiver is the `this`
   keyword, take the field's declaration from the access's symbol and look its binding up in `context.bindings` by that
   declaration's position, exactly as the identifier case does. When a binding exists the leaf carries it as
   `operandFlow` and carries **no** `operandParamName`, `operandPropertyPath` or `operandTypeRef`: a field is state of the
   instance, never an object-member read of a parameter, so the stub stitch must not see it. When no binding exists the
   leaf is built exactly as today.
2. Read nothing from text. Update the header comment: the new case and why a field leaf names no parameter.

## Tests

- `EDGE: this.config.mode (a property chain below this) => unchanged, still an object-member read`
- `EDGE: this.calls with no binding => built exactly as today`
