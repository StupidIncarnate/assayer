## Words used here

An **executor** is the function passed as the first argument of `new Promise(...)`. The language calls it in place, with two
functions: `resolve` and `reject`. It runs when the host that evaluates `new Promise` runs, and it reads the host's parameters
by closure, so the executor's branches are decided by the host's parameters.
