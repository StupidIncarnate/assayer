## Read first

`call-site-contract.ts`: its `position` field and the doc of `receiver` and `method`, which says a chained receiver leaves both
unset.

## Contract

An optional field `receiverPosition`, with the same schema `position` uses (line and column). It is the position of the call
expression that is the receiver of this call. In `load(count).then(a).catch(b)`, the `.catch` call's `receiverPosition` is the
`.then` call's `position`, and the `.then` call's is `load(count)`'s. It is absent when the receiver is not a call.

## What to implement

1. Add the field. Say in the header comment what it is for: a consumer follows it from a callback's call to the root of the
   chain (task 17.3.1).
2. Widen the sentence about a chained receiver: `method` is set for a chained receiver too, and `receiver` stays unset.
   Task 17.2.2 records these facts.
