## Contract

1. `kind` gains `'has-property'` and `'lacks-property'`. Both carry `literal`: the property name, a string. They mean
   `'x' in obj` holds (`has-property`) or does not hold (`lacks-property`). The operand is the object. A `!('x' in obj)`
   is the existing `not` connective over a `has-property` leaf, so `lacks-property` is what a leaf becomes when a
   violating arm asks for it; the reader emits `has-property` only (task 12.2.3). Keep `lacks-property` in the enum so
   a rule can name the violating side.
2. A new optional field `loose: z.boolean().optional()`. It is `true` only on an `eq` or `neq` leaf that came from `==`
   or `!=`. It is absent everywhere else. Edge-value placement (task 12.3.3) and the rule-named value (task 12.5.1)
   read it, because `'' == 0` is true while `'' === 0` is not.
3. Extend the PURPOSE block: one paragraph for the two `in` kinds, one for `loose`.

`predicate.stub.ts` needs no change: the new field is optional and the default kind is unchanged.
