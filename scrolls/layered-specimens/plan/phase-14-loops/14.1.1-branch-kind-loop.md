## Contract

1. Add `'loop'` to the `kind` enum. The enum then reads `if`, `switch`, `ternary`, `default`, `destructure-default`,
   `loop`. A `loop` branch carries `arms` (13.1.1): `['runs', 'skipped']` for `for`, `for…of`, `for…in` and `while`
   (the first label is the arm taken when the condition holds, so the body runs), and `['repeats', 'stops']` for
   `do…while`.
2. Update the PURPOSE block in plain words: what a `loop` branch's condition is and what its arm labels are.
3. The stub keeps its default.
